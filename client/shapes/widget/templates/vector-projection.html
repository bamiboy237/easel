Drop a perpendicular from the tip of a to the line of b; it meets the line at p. The arrow from the origin to p is the projection projb a, and its signed length, positive along b and negative against it, is compb a.

Try it: drag a across the dotted line and watch the sign of compb a. Then lengthen b: the projection stays put. Arrow keys move a clicked tip by 0.1 (Shift: 1).
